Every dispatching unit in AEMO’s registration list that we can place, grouped into the station it belongs to. Marker colour is the station’s largest fuel by registered capacity — the same palette as the fuel-mix chart; marker area scales with registered capacity, so a 2 GW station covers a hundred times the area of a 20 MW one rather than a hundred times the width. Drag to pan; ctrl/⌘ + scroll to zoom, or pick a region above. Grey jurisdictions (WA, NT) are separate grids and structurally outside the NEM.

A NEM region is electrical, not geographic. Snowy’s Murray Power Station sits in New South Wales but dispatches into VIC1, so filtering to Victoria pins it where it physically stands — on the wrong side of the border. Pins are the station’s real coordinates; regions are AEMO’s. Coastlines are drawn at 1:50 million, so a station on a headland or a border can sit a whisker outside the outline.

Pick a station on the map for its details, live output and emissions profile.
